# THE DOOR'S TOOLS -- what each one declares, and who may call it

GENERATED by `go test ./internal/tools -run TestEveryToolHasAPermissionRecord -update` (from `atlas/line`);
never hand-edited. It is read OFF the tool table and the shipped role policy, by the functions that judge a
real call (`rbac.Can`, `Tool.Writes`, `Tool.Reads`, `HeldExempt`), so it cannot say what the door does not
do; and a stale copy fails the test, so a tool added, a flag turned or a role changed shows up in review.
The core's WHAT'S LEFT D7, 2026-10-02.

**What it does not claim.** A tool's `Writes` flag is its author's declaration. Nothing here proves a tool
that says it only reads never writes; that is each tool's own test. The record makes a change of
declaration visible. It does not make a false one true.

## How a call is judged

1. WHO is asking comes from the transport, never from the arguments. The glass (the service wire) is the
   operator's hand and passes. Anything else is judged by the name the door gave it against the tenant's
   role policy (`rbac.Can`): the tool by name, then `*`, then the kinds the call carries -- `tools` always,
   and `edit` for a tool that writes or `read` for one that does not. A tenant with no roles assigned is
   open mode, and says so on every hold record and in the boot line.
2. With the door's `--auth` on, a call that WRITES from anything but the glass is parked as a hold and runs
   only when the operator approves it there. `hold_answer` and `hold_list` are exempt: they are how a hold
   is answered and read. A reading action of a writing tool (`git_tag list`) is judged as a read.
3. `can_approve` is structurally false. rbac never grants approval; it lives in the hand alone.
4. A tool declared `ServiceOnly` is the operator's own hand (`shell_run`, his typed shell; `aider_run` and `aider_undo`, his
   Aider; `law_add` and `law_seal`, his laws): `Registry.Call` refuses
   every caller but the glass before RBAC or the holds look at it, and writes the attempt down. No role grants it
   and no agent can park a call of it for him to approve, so the role columns below say what a role would be
   allowed WITHOUT that declaration and cannot make a caller other than the glass able to call it.
